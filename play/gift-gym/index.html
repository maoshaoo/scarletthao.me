How it’s builtOne web page, six angles, and nothing leaves your phone.
The idea
Good gift-givers aren’t born that way. They notice what people say, write it down, start early, and think from a few angles. This turns each of those into a habit.
The six angles
Upgrade something they use every day. Fix something that keeps annoying them. Treat them to the nice version. Do something together. Call back a memory. Feed their thing.
The warm-up
A made-up person each day, put together from a hand-written deck of relationships, hobbies, quirks, budgets and occasions. Three ideas in 60 seconds, then example answers to compare. Nothing’s graded.
The countdown
Each date starts three weeks out: pick by two weeks, buy by ten days, write the card two days before. Afterwards it asks how the gift landed.
Your data
People, clues, ideas and past gifts stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere. Anonymous counts of rounds played go to Umami, with no cookies and nothing you type.
