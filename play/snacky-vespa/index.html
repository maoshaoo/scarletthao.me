Play / Snacky Vespa 🚧 Work in progress

Snack first, route second. Introducing: Snacky Vespa.

Most days, I’m on my Vespa somewhere in San Francisco, always thinking about what to eat next.

With a dozen microclimates, the sunny side of the city changes every week.

Pick a snack, a start time, and how long you’ve got, and this plans a scenic adventure that catches some sun, or visits Karl the Fog.

Still a work in progress: some stops and routes haven’t been checked in person yet. Ride with care, and trust the road over the app.
