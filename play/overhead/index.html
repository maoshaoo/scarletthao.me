How it’s builtOne web page, a free community flight feed, and a bit of trigonometry.
The idea
I hear a plane, look up, and wonder where it’s going. This answers that, and tells you which way to face and how high to look so you can actually spot it.
The data
Planes broadcast their position, altitude and speed over ADS-B. Volunteers with antennas share what they hear with adsb.lol, which this checks every 10 seconds. airplanes.live and adsb.fi are backups.
Routes
A plane only broadcasts its callsign, not where it’s going. Origin and destination come from a separate route lookup and can be missing or out of date.
Where to look
The direction is the compass bearing from you to the plane. The angle up is its altitude against its distance along the ground.
Your data
Your location and saved places stay in this browser. Only rounded coordinates go to the flight feed. A typed address goes to OpenStreetMap to find it on the map.
